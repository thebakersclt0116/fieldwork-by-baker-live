The 2027 templates are version 06/2026; the 2022 templates are version 08/2023. Copyright remains with BACB. The applicable requirements must be selected by the trainee and supervisor, rather than inferred from the year of an entry. Baker fills identity and recorded hours but never signs or certifies a form. Both parties must review and sign. Generate new filled forms when BACB updates a template; existing signed copies must retain their original contents.
